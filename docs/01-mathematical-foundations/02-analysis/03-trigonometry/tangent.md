# Tangent

## TL;DR

The **tangent function** is a trigonometric function defined as the ratio of the opposite side to the adjacent side in a right triangle.

$$
\tan(\theta)=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}
$$

It can also be expressed using sine and cosine:

$$
\tan(\theta)=\frac{\sin(\theta)}{\cos(\theta)}
$$

## Definition

The tangent function is defined for all real numbers except

$$
x=\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi,\qquad k\in\mathbb{Z}
$$

At these points, $\cos(x)=0$, so the tangent is undefined.

## Important Properties

### Periodicity

Tangent has period $\pi$:

$$
\tan(x+\pi)=\tan(x)
$$

### Odd Function

Tangent is an odd function:

$$
\tan(-x)=-\tan(x)
$$

### Derivative

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\tan(x)=\sec^2(x)
$$

### Integral

$$
\int\tan(x),dx=-\ln|\cos(x)|+C
$$

## Important Values

| $x$             |            $\tan(x)$ |
| --------------- | -------------------: |
| $0$             |                  $0$ |
| $\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ |
| $\frac{\pi}{4}$ |                  $1$ |
| $\frac{\pi}{3}$ |           $\sqrt{3}$ |
| $\frac{\pi}{2}$ |            undefined |
| $\pi$           |                  $0$ |

## Asymptotes

Tangent has vertical asymptotes at

$$
x=\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi,\qquad k\in\mathbb{Z}
$$

Near these points, $\tan(x)$ grows without bound.

## Relation to Sine and Cosine

Tangent is the quotient of sine and cosine:

$$
\tan(x)=\frac{\sin(x)}{\cos(x)}
$$

Therefore, tangent is undefined whenever cosine is zero.

## Applications

Tangent is commonly used in:

* Geometry and trigonometry
* Calculating slopes and angles
* Physics and engineering
* Computer graphics
* Navigation
* Calculus and differential equations
